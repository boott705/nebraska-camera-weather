Collection at a glance

Nebraska 511 road cameras are photographed every 15 minutes (the counts below say how many). Each frame is matched to the airport weather reports (ASOS) that describe the air at that moment.

Cameras and weather stations

Click a camera to see its latest frame, its nearest airport, and how its picture has changed over the recent window. Cameras with no airport within 50 km are hollow. Green triangles are NDOT road weather stations (air and pavement temperature and wind; they report no visibility, so each camera's panel shows its nearest road sensor's raw readings next to the airport's). Amber bars are NDOT message signs: 511 lists a sign only while it shows a message, so signs are added as they switch on (VAS speed signs along I-80, mile 0–248, appear during reduced-speed events).

Day to night on I-80

Twelve I-80 cameras from the Wyoming line to Omaha, one frame per hour for the newest 24 hours. Pick one, then drag the slider or press Play: the time, the sun's elevation and the visibility the nearest airport reported change with each frame.

Camera moves: before and after

Times a camera was panned, tilted, zoomed, or pointed at a different scene, found by matching fixed details (poles, signs, lane lines) between frames of the same view. Drag the slider across each picture to compare the frame before the move with the frame after it. Only daytime frames are compared, so a move made overnight shows between the last evening frame and the first morning frame. Cameras that sway slightly in the wind, and changes that fog, rain or passing trucks cause for a frame or two, are not counted.

Weather and matching

A visibility report at time T averages the 10 minutes ending at T, so a frame is matched to the first report within 10 minutes after it ("averaging window"). If none exists, the closest report within 30 minutes is used. Matches keep improving for 4 hours as late reports arrive; after that they are final.

Road weather stations: raw readings

Values as the NDOT road stations sent them over the recent window, not checked against anything; no station sends visibility. A highlighted cell is "mostly one value": at least half its readings are the same number (−22 °F is exactly −30 °C; 32 °F is exactly 0 °C). Hover a cell for its details.

NDOT message signs

Every electronic sign 511 has listed since recording began, with everything 511 reports about it. 511 lists a sign only while it shows a message, so a sign that drops off the list is recorded as "not listed" (most likely blank). Variable advisory speed (VAS) signs along I-80 appear here the first time they show a speed. Click a row to see the sign on the map; open "History" to see each message change.
